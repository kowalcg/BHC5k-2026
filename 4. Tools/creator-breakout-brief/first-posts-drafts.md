> Drafts for the four P1 concepts that don't require external casting.
> C02 (Dad + Kids 1K) is not drafted here — it needs a recruited family
> first (see `03-creator-archetypes.md` casting call).

**How to use this file:** Each section below is a ready-to-ship post. Copy
the caption into your platform, generate the image using the matching

## C01 · Training Diary — Week 1 Post

**Owner:** Liz (producer) + recruited Bronte Runners member (face)
**Format:** 30–60s vertical reel, phone-shot, one take preferred
**Channel:** IG Reels (primary) → cross-post to FB Reels
**Ship by:** April 28, 2026 (assumes recruiting done by April 24)

### Video outline (record this)

1. **Opening (0–3s):** Face-cam, walking or on a run. Hook line spoken flatly,
   no music yet.
2. **Hook line (literal script):**
   > "Hi — I'm [NAME]. I'm [AGE]. Six months ago I couldn't run to the end
   > of my street. On June 21, I'm running the first Bronte Harbour
   > Classic 5K. This is week one."
3. **B-roll beat (3–8s):** Lacing shoes / leaving the house / first
   intervals. No polish.
4. **Middle (8–25s):** One specific thing that happened this week. A
   struggle, a small win, a moment of doubt. Voice-over or face-cam.
   Example: "My plan said run 1 minute, walk 2. I made it to 45 seconds
   and had to stop. That's where I'm at."
5. **Close (25–40s):**
   > "Ten weeks to go. I'll see you here next week. If you're thinking
   > about signing up, do it with me."

### Caption (IG/FB)

> Six months ago I couldn't run to the end of my street. On June 21 I'm
> running my first 5K — the first Bronte Harbour Classic. 🟥
>
> This is week 1.
>
> I'll be here every week until race day. If you're thinking about
> signing up but haven't, do it with me. It's scarier alone.
>
> 🏃 Register → bronteharbourclassic.com
> 📍 Bronte Harbour Park · Father's Day · June 21, 2026
>
> #BronteHarbourClassic #FirstFiveK #CouchTo5K #OakvilleRuns
> #FathersDayRace #BronteRunners #RoadToBronte

### Why this works (reference for the team)

- Kills O1 (not a real runner) and D1 (want to feel like a runner) because
  the speaker *is* the audience, not a coach talking about them.
- "Six months ago I couldn't run to the end of my street" is a specific,
  checkable claim — no superlatives, no hype. Trust signal.
- Weekly cadence is the entire point. **Do not ship Week 1 until you have
  commitment for Weeks 2–10.**

  Three rows, monospace.

- Strong confidence tier: deadline-based email with a real dollar amount

  should decide for you") builds trust and differentiates from every
  other race that crank-calls their list.
- Repeatable template. Swap dates, swap numbers, re-send. Add to weekly
  rhythm.
