## Brand truths (what is uniquely true about us?)

These are the things only *we* can say. Every piece of content should
ladder up to at least one.

1. **T1 — The only Father's Day race-festival on the Oakville waterfront.**
   Nobody else in the GTA owns this exact intersection of day + place + format.
2. **T2 — Born from a running club, not a promoter.** Bronte Runners Club
   started this. It's a community event with a professional chip-timing
   backbone, not a white-label promo.
3. **T3 — A 5K *and* a festival, not a 5K *then* everyone leaves.** Kids 1K,
   live music, vendors, food, kids zone. The day is the product — the race is
   the reason to show up.
4. **T4 — Flat, fast, scenic waterfront.** PR-friendly for experienced runners,
   beginner-friendly for first-timers. Same course serves both.
5. **T5 — Founding year stakes.** Every photo, every result, every sponsor
   logo is literally "the first one." Scarcity of moment, not scarcity of seats.
6. **T6 — Charity with a human face.** Oakville Dads Charity ties the Father's
   Day framing to actual community impact — not just logo placement.
7. **T7 — Local business ecosystem behind it.** TapeGeeks, GearTOP, Pace
   Performance, Bronte Runners, Town of Oakville. This is a real Oakville
   production, not an import.

## Buyer objections (what's stopping each audience from registering?)

Organized by segment. These are the exact doubts we need to preempt in
content.

### 🎯 First-time 5K runners (biggest growth segment)
- **O1.** "I'm not a real runner. I'll be last. Everyone will judge me."
- **O2.** "5K sounds far. I've never done that distance. What if I can't?"
- **O3.** "I don't know what to wear / what to bring / where to park."
- **O4.** "Is $45 worth it for me if I'm just walking it?"

### 🏃 Experienced local runners (credibility buyers)
- **O5.** "It's an inaugural. Will it be chaos? Wrong distance? Bad timing?"
- **O6.** "Is the course fast enough to PR on?"
- **O7.** "I already run in Oakville every weekend. Why pay for a 5K here?"

### 👨‍👧 Dads with kids (Father's Day wedge)
- **O8.** "I want to run *with* my kid, not leave them at the finish line."
- **O9.** "Father's Day is supposed to be about relaxing, not logistics."
- **O10.** "My kid has never done a race. Will they be overwhelmed?"

### 🏡 Local Oakville / Burlington / Mississauga families
- **O11.** "What do the non-runners in the family do for three hours?"
- **O12.** "Is this actually good, or is it the first-year mess?"
- **O13.** "Is parking going to be a nightmare?"

### 🏢 GTA-wider runners (Toronto / Mississauga commute)
- **O14.** "Why drive 45 minutes for a 5K when I have one down the street?"
- **O15.** "Is the scenery worth the trip?"

### 🏅 Running clubs & corporate teams
- **O16.** "Can we sign up as a team and run together?"
- **O17.** "What do we get for a group entry?"

## Desires (what does each audience actually *want* — emotionally, not rationally?)

- **D1.** *"I want to feel like a runner."* (First-timers, ~35–50, mostly women in
  Bronte Runners orbit.) → Proof that people like them finished.
- **D2.** *"I want a race-day memory my kid will talk about for years."* (Dads)
  → Picture of kid crossing finish line wearing a medal.
- **D3.** *"I want my Sunday morning to feel like a vacation, not an errand."*
  (Local families) → Festival atmosphere, not parking-lot vibe.
- **D4.** *"I want to belong to Bronte, even if I'm not from here."* (GTA
  runners) → Local-insider content that makes them feel they've already been
  there.
- **D5.** *"I want a time I can post on Strava."* (Experienced runners) →
  Course math + previous flats-on-waterfront PR stories.
- **D6.** *"I want to support something local without writing a cheque."*
  (Community-first buyers) → Charity tie-in made specific, not abstract.
- **D7.** *"I want to be early on a good thing."* (Sponsors, founding-year
  runners) → Inaugural framing; "you were there" social capital.

## Anti-positioning (what we will NOT say)

The Breakout Kit calls these "anti-positioning blocks." Generic approaches we
explicitly reject. If a draft sounds like one of these, rewrite it.

- ❌ **"Come run 5K in Oakville."** — No hook, no stakes, no reason to care.
- ❌ **"Most scenic race in Ontario."** — Unverifiable superlative; every race
  claims this.
- ❌ **"Bring the whole family."** — A platitude, not a promise. Replace with
  "Kids 1K at 8:45, medals for every kid, jumping castle runs 9–12."
- ❌ **"New race you don't want to miss."** — Passive, fear-based, bland.
  Replace with inaugural-year *specificity*: "The first finishers' medal. The
  first sponsor wall. The first Bronte Harbour Classic."
- ❌ **Elite-runner photography where the PR is the point.** — We are 80%
  first-timers, walkers, dads, and kids. Show *them*, not sub-20 finishers.
- ❌ **Generic "register now" CTAs with no deadline/price change urgency.**
  Always pair CTA with current tier + date of next increase.
- ❌ **Copy-pasting Toronto Waterfront Marathon's tone.** They're an elite
  international race. We're a Father's Day community festival. Different
  product, different voice.

## Voice & tone

| Attribute | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | Warm, local, earned confidence | Hyped, corporate, exclamation-heavy |
| Authority | "Chip-timed, Town-approved, built by runners" | "#1 best race ever" |
| Humor | Dad-joke-adjacent, self-aware about being inaugural | Sarcastic, snarky |
| Inclusivity | First-timers and walkers treated as co-stars | First-timers as an afterthought |
| Urgency | Price-tier dates, spot counts | "LAST CHANCE!" banners |
