# 04 · Concept Slate — Twelve Scored Cards

> Stage 4 of 5. Every concept here is a **testable idea**, not a finished
> post. Scoring uses the Creator Breakout Kit's four dimensions (adapted
> from their framework). Do not attempt all 12. The top 3 go into `05-test-plan.md`.

## Scoring rubric (how to read the cards)

Each concept is scored 1–5 on four dimensions:

| Dimension | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategic Fit** (how well it ladders to brand truths + objections) | Off-brand or solves a non-problem | Kills a top-5 objection and hits a T1–T3 truth |
| **Breakout Evidence** (does the pattern work elsewhere?) | Hypothesis, zero comparables | Strong pattern — multiple outliers logged in 02 |
| **Production Ease** (how hard to make) | Needs hired crew + paid tools | Phone, one person, one hour |
| **Confidence** (how sure we are this will work *for us*) | **Hypothesis** | **Strong** |

Priority is the honest gut call after weighing all four. `P1` = launch now.
`P2` = launch after P1 reports. `P3` = hold for Phase 2 or drop.
