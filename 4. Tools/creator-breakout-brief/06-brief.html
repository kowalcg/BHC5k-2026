Bronte Harbour Classic.

Creator Breakout Brief — organic-first marketing plan for the inaugural Father's Day race + festival.

INAUGURAL 5K + Kids 1K Sun Jun 21, 2026 · 8:00 AM Bronte Harbour Park, Oakville Target: 800+ Charity: Oakville Dads

01.Executive Summary

We are running an organic-first, evidence-driven content plan for the inaugural Bronte Harbour Classic. The single most ownable angle is the only Father's Day race + festival on the Oakville waterfront — no other GTA race can say all three at once.

Launch wave is 5 concepts across 5 archetypes, shipping between Apr 20 and May 5. Winners get doubled-down on in Phase 2 (May 15–Jun 14). Paid amplification (Meta + Google) is optional, Phase 3, and only runs against creative that already has organic signal.

Highest-leverage play: C01 Training Diary Series (Peer Finisher archetype) — lowest production cost, highest compounding emotional return, kills the biggest objection for the biggest segment (first-time 5K runners).
