# 05 · Test Plan — What We Launch First and How We Know It Works

> Stage 5 of 5. Five P1 concepts from `04-concept-slate.md` become the
> launch wave. This page is the operational plan: who owns what, when it
> ships, what counts as a win, when we kill it, and how paid ads bolt on
> *after* organic has proved signal.

## Golden rule

**Never run paid ads against unvalidated creative.** The Creator Breakout
Kit's core thesis: prove the angle works organically (or in a very cheap
test), *then* pour spend into the winners. Running paid against C01 in
week one is the default mistake we're explicitly avoiding.

## Success metrics (organic)

Measure at 14 days post-launch and again at 28 days. Compare to the
**account's own baseline** — NOT industry averages. An outlier is 3×
your median.

### Concept-level (per concept)

| Metric | Baseline source | Win threshold | Kill threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach / impressions | IG/FB Insights last 30d median | **≥3×** median | <1× median |
| Saves + shares | IG/FB Insights | **≥3×** median | <0.5× median |
| Profile visits | IG Insights | ≥2× median | Flat |
| Comment quality | Qualitative | Real questions about registration | Silence or bot comments |
| Link clicks (if applicable) | Bitly / UTM | ≥100 in 14d | <25 |

### Campaign-level (the whole wave together)

| Metric | Source | Target (by May 15) |
|---|---|---|
| Race Roster registrations attributed to organic | Race Roster + UTMs | +75 over the 30-day window |
| Newsletter signups | Site form | +50 |
| IG follower growth | IG Insights | +15% |
| FB page followers | FB Insights | +10% |
| Featured press / repost | Manual log | ≥1 local publication or influencer repost |

### Kill criteria (pull the plug, don't iterate forever)

- **C01 Training Diary** — if week-2 post is <50% of week-1's reach, the
  parasocial bond isn't forming. Re-cast or drop.
- **C02 Dad + Kids 1K** — if the first post lands below account median at
  all, the hook is wrong. Reshoot with a different family before iterating
  format.
- **C04 Build-Out** — hardest to kill because it compounds. Only kill if
  4 consecutive posts land below median.
- **C05 Urgency email** — if open rate <25% or CTR <2%, the subject line
  is wrong, not the concept. Iterate subjects, not concept.
- **C06 Local windows** — if businesses don't repost, the pattern dies
  silently. Log it, move on.

## Phase 2 — Winners only (May 15 → Jun 14)

After 4 weeks of organic signal, do three things and only three things:

1. **Double down on the one winning concept.** Whichever P1 hit win
   thresholds, produce 2–3 more in the same pattern. Don't experiment;
   scale the thing that worked.
2. **Launch the P2 support cast** that pairs with the winner. If C01 won,
   add C07 (coach course explainer) to build the beginner-to-PR bridge.
   If C02 won, add C12 (charity story) to deepen the emotional moat.
3. **Kill everything else.** Do not carry losers into Phase 2 out of
   politeness. The marketing tracker (MKT-001 through MKT-015) has enough
   commitments without zombie content.

---

## Phase 3 — Paid amplification (optional, Jun 1 → Jun 20)

**Only if organic has proved one winner.** Paid in this plan is about
**amplifying existing winning creative** to audiences that wouldn't have
seen it organically — not about running cold creative against a cold
audience.

### Budget tiers (choose one based on sponsor cash available)

| Budget | Platforms | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **$0** | — | Skip phase 3. We didn't prove enough. |
| **$250** | Meta (FB + IG) only | Boost the single best-performing organic post as an ad. 14 days. Objective: traffic to bronteharbourclassic.com registration page. |
| **$750** | Meta + Google Search | Above + Google Search on "5k race oakville", "oakville father's day event", "bronte harbour race". 14 days. |
| **$1,500+** | Meta + Google + IG partnerships | Above + 1 paid micro-influencer post from Oakville/Burlington running community (~10k followers, ~$300). |

### Meta targeting (copy-paste)

- **Geo:** Oakville, Burlington, Mississauga, Hamilton, Etobicoke, Milton
  (25 km radius around Bronte Harbour Park).
- **Age:** 30–60 (split test 30–45 vs 45–60 if budget allows).
- **Interests:** Running, Parkrun, Half marathon, Father's Day, Oakville,
  Kids activities.
- **Lookalikes:** 1% LAL of Bronte Runners Club IG/FB followers (if the
  audience is large enough — need ≥1,000 source).
- **Exclusions:** Anyone who already registered (upload Race Roster
  export weekly).

### Google Search (copy-paste)

- **Exact-match keywords:** `[5k race oakville]`, `[father's day race oakville]`,
  `[bronte harbour classic]`, `[oakville kids race]`.
- **Broad-match negatives:** -triathlon, -marathon, -half.
- **Landing page:** `bronteharbourclassic.com/races/5k/` (not homepage).
- **Daily cap:** $15 until we see CTR > 4%, then raise.

### Paid kill criteria

- If CPM > $15 and CTR < 1% after 4 days → pause and swap creative.
- If cost per registration > $30 → pause and reconsider. The 5K entry is
  $45–62; spending >$30 to acquire one burns margin.

## What this plan does NOT cover

- Partnership activations (Robbie Burns race, gyms, elementary schools) —
  these are in the marketing tracker already, separate workflow.
- Press releases and media outreach — belongs in the media kit / PR
  workflow, not this brief.
- Sponsor fulfillment posts (logo placement, co-branded content) —
  separate from the acquisition funnel; handled by sponsor-relations lead.
- Race-day operations content (live posts, finish-line photo workflow) —
  belongs in the race-day run-of-show.
