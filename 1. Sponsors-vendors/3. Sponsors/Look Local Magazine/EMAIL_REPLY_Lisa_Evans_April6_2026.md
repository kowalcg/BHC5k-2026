## Greg's Answers — April 6, 2026

**To:** writelisaevans32@gmail.com
**Subject:** Re: Bronte Harbour Classic - Look Local Info Request

Hi Lisa,

Happy to answer by email — here we go:

**1. Do you need to be an experienced runner to participate?**
Not at all. The Bronte Harbour Classic is designed for everyone — from first-timers to competitive runners. The 5K distance is approachable for anyone who wants to get off the couch and try something new, and we have a Kids 1K Fun Run for the little ones too. If you can walk, you can participate. We want people to show up, experience the energy, and leave feeling like they belong in this community.

**2. What makes community / charity runs different from a regular race?**
In a regular race, you're competing. In a community run, you're connecting. The Bronte Harbour Classic is organized by the **Bronte Runners Club** — a community running group that has been deeply involved in charitable events across Oakville for years. Last year alone, our members were the **largest community team donors to the Terry Fox Foundation**, raising close to **$27,000**. That culture of giving back is in our DNA. When there's a charitable component, the finish line matters, but so does everything around it — the conversations on the course, the volunteers cheering you on, the families waiting at the finish. It's a shared sense of purpose that elevates the whole experience.

**3. What motivates most participants to sign up?**
It's rarely just one thing. For some it's a fitness goal — a reason to get off the couch and train. For others it's social — their running club is doing it, or they want to do something meaningful with their family on Father's Day. And for many, the community giving aspect seals the deal. When people know their entry fee is going back into the neighbourhood, it feels less like a race and more like a cause worth being part of.

**4. Why do charity runs appeal to people who don't consider themselves "runners"?**
Because the identity shifts. You're not signing up to be a runner — you're signing up to do something good. That removes a huge psychological barrier. Nobody feels out of place when the reason you're there is bigger than your pace.

**5. What makes a run event successful, and what makes the Bronte Harbour Classic special?**
Success is when people finish and immediately want to come back next year. That happens when every detail is handled with care — the course is scenic, the volunteers are enthusiastic, the post-race experience is memorable. The Bronte Harbour Classic is a true community effort: organized by the Bronte Runners Club, supported by an incredible sponsor family led by **Mercedes-Benz Oakville** as our title sponsor, and backed by community partners who are genuinely invested in making this work. The location alone sets the tone — Bronte Harbour Park on a June morning is stunning. Add a festival atmosphere, live music, food trucks, a beer garden, and you have something people will talk about. We're building a Father's Day tradition for Oakville.

**6. How does the event benefit the community?**
Charitable donations are collected directly through the race registration page — participants can choose to donate when they sign up, making it simple and transparent. One hundred percent of those donations go to the **Oakville Dads Community Fund (ODCF)** and local Oakville community causes.

The Oakville Dads are a remarkable organization — mostly fathers who have been quietly supporting the local community for many years. They champion local food banks, families in need, and grassroots Oakville causes that don't always get the spotlight they deserve. They are exactly the kind of organization we wanted beside us for this event.

What's also special is the timing. This race falls on **Father's Day** — so supporting a charity led by fathers, for the community, on that particular Sunday just feels right on every level. And true to form, the Oakville Dads aren't just the charity recipient — they're active partners in making this event happen. Their team is on the ground with us, helping coordinate volunteers and supporting race day operations. It's a genuine two-way relationship, not just a logo on a banner.

**7. What would you say to someone who is hesitant to sign up?**
I'd say: the hardest part is registering. Once you do, you've made a commitment to yourself and your community — and that feeling carries you to the start line. You don't have to be fast. You don't even have to know how to run yet.

If you want to get started, come join us. The Bronte Runners go out **every Saturday and Sunday at 8:00 AM at the Bronte Harbour Gazebo** — that's where the fun begins. New members join us every week, and we welcome everyone. You'll know us when you see us — we're usually the largest group out there, and we call it the **red wave**. If you have performance goals, our coach will help you get there. If you just want good company and a reason to get outside, that works too.

You can find us through the race website at **bronteharbourclassic.com** or on **Facebook**. Just drop by one Saturday or Sunday morning — no registration, no commitment, just show up and run with us.
