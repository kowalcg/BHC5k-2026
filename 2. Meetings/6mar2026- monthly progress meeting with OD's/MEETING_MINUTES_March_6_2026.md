## 1. OCF Banking & Donation Setup

**Status:** Donations are live on both Race Roster and the Oakville Dads donation page, but funds are being held pending receipt of OCF banking information.

**Key points:**
- Laurie Armitage (Director of Finance, OCF) contacted Per about the tax receipt process
- OCF will manually generate receipts for donors; minimum donation amount for a receipt will be $20 (standard; not $50 as OCF suggested)
- Forced donations at registration were discussed and rejected — research and feedback from other organizers confirms this creates a poor participant experience
- Donations will be transferred to the OCF account on a weekly basis once banking info is connected
- Race Roster will provide a weekly spreadsheet of donors

**Two donation paths on the landing page:**
1. **Race Roster registration flow** — donor selects an amount at checkout (preset options from $25 to $500); expected to generate smaller donations on average
2. **Direct OCF link** (featured on the landing page) — goes directly to the OCF fund page; expected to capture larger one-time donations; tax receipts handled automatically by OCF software
