## Partnership Agreement

We're excited to confirm **Oakville Dads Community Fund (ODCF)** as the official charity partner for the inaugural **Bronte Harbour Classic 5K** on **Father's Day, June 21, 2026**.

This partnership aligns perfectly with both organizations' community values and the Father's Day timing.

---

## Event Overview

**Race Details:**
- Date: Sunday, June 21, 2026 (Father's Day)
- Location: Coronation Park, Bronte
- Race: 5K + 1K Kids Race
- Start Time: 8:00 AM
- Target: 800+ participants
- Current Registration: 80 runners

**Festival Component (9:30 AM - 3:00 PM):**
- 50+ vendor booths
- Live music at gazebo
- Kids activities (bouncy castle, face painting)

- Family-friendly Father's Day celebration

**Additional Exposure:**
- Part of new Halton 5K Race Series (with Moon & June and Canada Day races)

## Fundraising Structure

**Registration Donation Option:**
- Runners will see donation buttons during Race Roster registration ($5, $10, $20 options)
- Separate from registration fee - 100% goes to ODCF
- Comparable races average $5-20 per runner
- Example: Chilli Half Marathon raised $13,000 through this feature alone
- All donations receive charitable tax receipt through Oakville Community Foundation

**On-Site Fundraising:**
- Oakville Dads will have dedicated tent space at festival
- Opportunities for additional fundraising throughout the day

## Volunteer Requirements

**Critical Need: Race Operations (7:00-9:30 AM)**
- **60 volunteers needed**
- **Must be 18+ years old** (safety requirement for road marshals)
- **Time commitment:** 2.5 hours (7:00 or 7:30 AM - 9:30 AM)
- Grade 12 students can receive volunteer hours

**Roles:**
- Road marshals (street closures - most critical)
- Course management
- Water stations
- Bag check
- Finish line support (medals, water)
- Signage placement (~10 people)

**Strategy:**
Focus volunteer recruitment on the morning shift only. Additional festival support (9:30 AM - 3:00 PM) can be organized in 2-hour shifts if volunteers can stay longer.

**Early Setup (Optional):**
5:30-7:00 AM vendor coordination support if available.

**Perks:**
- Free coffee and refreshments
- Volunteer recognition
- Community service hours (for students)
