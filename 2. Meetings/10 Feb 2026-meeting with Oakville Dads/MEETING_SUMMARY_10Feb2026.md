## 2. About Oakville Dads - Structure & Charity Work

### Organizational Structure (Two Entities)
1. **Oakville Dads Inc.**
   - Runs golf tournaments, whiskey tastings, and social events
   - Not the charitable entity

2. **Oakville Dads Community Fund (ODCF)**
   - Created under Oakville Community Foundation
   - Has its own chair
   - Uses OCF's charitable number for tax receipts
   - Can donate to any registered charity in Canada
   - Can support universities for scholarships

### Primary Charitable Focus
**Theme:** Food insecurity, poverty, and homelessness (local, non-religious, not gender-based)

**Three Main Charity Partners:**
1. **Oakville Fair Share Food Bank** (primary charity)
   - Do food drives regularly
   - One of the largest donors

2. **Oakville Meals on Wheels**
   - Supporting seniors and shut-ins
   - Food insecurity focus
   - Top donor status

3. **Safety Net**
   - New partnership this year
   - Serves Halton and Peel regions
   - Supports underprivileged, newcomers, poverty relief
   - Has housing component
   - Physical location in Oakville (also serves some Mississauga)

### Scholarship Program
- **4 scholarships per year** to Oakville kids
- $2,000 each ($8,000 total)
- Past years found matching money to boost to $3,000 each

### Fundraising Success Track Record
**Historical Performance:**
- Started with Lighthouse charity: $400 first year → $700
- Switched to Terry Fox Run:
  - Year 1: $4,000
  - Year 2: $7,000
  - This year: **$23,000** (almost $27,000 final)

**Group Size:** Nearly 700 members (men and women)

### Give Oakville Partnership
- Oakville Dads donate during **Give Oakville** (November-December)
- Allows them to get matching money from Oakville Community Foundation
- Strategic approach to leverage donations

## 3. Bronte Harbour Classic 5K Race - Event Overview

### Race Background & Vision
- **Founding Principle:** "For the love of running"
- Goal to build community beyond just Bronte Runners
- Model: **Business + Community + Charity**
- First inaugural year after 3-year planning process

### Race Participants & Membership
- **Bronte Runners Group:** ~700 members (men and women)
- Age requirement: Under 13 must have parent with them
- Very social group focused on community

### Race Details
**Current Year (2026):**
- **5K race** (starting distance approved by city)
- **1K for kids**
- Target: **800+ runners**
- Currently: **80 people already registered**

**Future Plans:**
- Add **10K race** next year
- Goal: Become **Canadian sanctioned race**
- Positioning: **"Fastest race in Canada for 5K and 10K"**

### Sanctioning Process Requirements
- Course must be **flat**
- Elevations must be proven
- Independent study required to verify course is flat and fast
- Sanctioned through **Athletics Ontario** and **Athletics Canada** (Greg is a member)
- Greg is a **former national team member** and trainer

### Race Course Routes

**5K Route (2026):**
- Start: Coronation Park
- Route: Ontario Street → Marine Drive → Bronte Road loop
- **Note:** Route may change depending on construction at park parking lot
- Still tentative - waiting on city confirmation about construction timing
- Backup route (original plan) also loops through parking lot

**10K Route (2027):**
- Extended route down Lakeshore to Appleby College area
- Loop onto Rebecca Street
- Return via 4th Line → Lakeshore → back to start

**Road Closures:**
- One lane closure (not full street closure)
- Some through traffic continues
- Extensive signage throughout race course
- Church groups being approached to cheer at various points

### Prize Money & Elite Runners
- **Cash prizes for winners**
- Already have **one elite runner signed up**
- Elite 5K time: **13-14 minutes**
- Cash prizes attract competitive runners

### Registration & Pricing
**Tiered Pricing Structure:**
- Currently: **$73** (early bird)
- Will increase to **$81**
- Standard pricing for Ontario races ($70-80 range)
- **Note:** Pricing structure will change next year due to Athletics Ontario changes

### Race Day Technology
- **Timing chips:** Bibs have chips that activate at start/finish line
- Cameras and timing software for accurate results
- Professional race management system

## 5. Race Day Schedule & Event Details

### Timeline
- **5:00-5:30 AM:** Setup begins, vendor arrival and setup
- **7:00 AM:** Ontario Street closes to traffic
- **7:00-7:30 AM:** Volunteer arrival (critical race volunteers)
- **8:00 AM:** Race start
- **9:30 AM:** Race completion (approximately)
- **Post-race → 3:00 PM:** Festival/vendor event continues

### Vendor Event / Festival Component
**Location:** Entire Coronation Park (by the gazebo/butterfly area)

**Festival Features:**
- **50+ vendor booths**
- Live music bands at gazebo
- Food trucks
- Jumping castle for kids
- Face painting
- Dog walk component (see separate section)
- Beer tent (95% confirmed)
- Oakville Dads will have their own tent space

**Vendor Setup Requirements:**
- Entry: 5:00-7:00 AM (before street closure)
- City inspector checks all tents
- **Important:** Tents must be **weighted down** (not staked)
  - Learned from 2023 Latino event tornado incident
  - City requires sandbags or weights (can't spike tents into grass)
- Food trucks park in designated city-approved area

**Atmosphere & Community Impact:**
- Whole family event celebrating Father's Day
- Different from races where people finish and leave immediately
- Festival creates all-day community gathering
- Expected **~2,000 people total** throughout day
- Local businesses benefit (restaurants, shops, etc.)

### Noise & Permits
- Music and noise already approved by city as part of permit
- All speakers must **face toward the lake** (away from residential)
- Away from most residential areas (mainly condos)
- Band shell normally hosts events

## 6. Volunteer Requirements & Coordination

### Critical Race Day Volunteers (7:00-9:30 AM)
**Primary Need:** **~60 volunteers** for race operations
- **Age Requirement:** Must be **18+ years old** (cannot use kids for safety)
- **Time Commitment:** 7:00/7:30 AM - 9:30 AM (2-3 hours)
- Can give volunteer hours to **Grade 12 students**

**Strategy Discussed:**
- Focus recruitment on this critical morning time slot ONLY
- If given multiple time options, everyone picks later times and no one volunteers for 7 AM
- After securing morning volunteers, can ask if people can stay longer
- Bronte Runners will volunteer after racing
- Can recruit volunteers' spouses and 18+ children

**Volunteer Roles & Responsibilities:**
1. **Road marshals** - manning street closures (critical)
2. Course management - keeping runners on course
3. Water stations - setup and management
4. Bag check - managing runner belongings
5. Medal and water distribution at finish
6. Signage crew - ~10 people to place sponsor signs around course
7. General course support

### Early Morning Setup Volunteers (5:30-7:00 AM)
**Secondary Need:** Vendor coordination team
- Help coordinate vendor setup and positioning
- Ensure vendors set up in correct locations
- Coordinate measurements and layout
- Monitor city compliance (tent weighting, etc.)
- Prevent vehicle entry after 7 AM closure

**Estimated Need:** Additional volunteers for this shift (number TBD)

### Festival Volunteers (9:30 AM - 3:00 PM)
- Part of the 100-person volunteer pool
- Less critical than morning race volunteers
- Can be organized in **2-hour shifts** (works well based on food drive experience)
- May need fewer volunteers during this period

### Total Volunteer Goal
