A site map was reviewed during the meeting. Key layout decisions discussed:

- **Kids Zone:** Moved to the bottom-right corner, near the **water feature** in the park, so children can access the water and the kids zone together. Note: the kids zone and water feature may need to be cordoned off from each other for safety.
- **Beer Garden:** Originally near the kids zone; now being repositioned. Two proposals discussed:
