## 2. Route Planning and Road Closures

### Proposed Route:

- **Start Location:** Bronte Heritage Waterfront Park (intersection of Jones and Ontario Street, near gazebo)
- **Route Description:**
  - East on Ontario Street
  - Marine Drive towards Coronation Park
  - Loop around Coronation Park (using East Parking Lot area by the lake)
  - Return via Lakeshore Road
  - Back onto Marine Drive to Jones Street
  - South into Bronte Heritage Park (finish)

### Road Closure Requirements:

- **Ontario Street:** Full closure from Jones to Marine Drive
- **Marine Drive:** Running lane only (not full closure)
- **Lakeshore Road:** Southbound lane only from Belvedere to East Parking Lot

### Start/Finish Line Configuration:

- **Start Line:** Intersection of Ontario and Nelson Streets
- **Finish Line:** Jones Street heading south (Jones and Ontario intersection)
- **Distance Between Start/Finish:** Approximately 400 meters apart (due to route measurement requirements)

### Route Modifications from Original Plan:

- Original plan included loop around condo complex on Bronte Road
- Revised to avoid Bronte Road blockages as requested by town
- Larger loop inside Coronation Park utilizing lakeside path and East Parking Lot

## 6. Noise Bylaws and Amplified Sound

### Event Needs:

- Amplified sound required from 7:00 AM onwards
- Live music performance throughout the day until 3:00 PM
- Sound system for race announcements and event coordination

### Noise Bylaw Considerations:

- Standard noise bylaw may restrict amplified sound before 7:00 AM or 8:00 AM (to be confirmed)
- Two pathways for noise exemption:
  1. **Noise Exemption Permit** from Municipal Enforcement Services (MES)
  2. **Special Event Permit Exemption** granted by Director of Parks and Open Spaces

### Recommended Approach:

- Noise exemption to be written into Special Event Permit conditions
- Granted on authority of Director of Parks and Open Spaces (most likely pathway)
- Anna to confirm exact bylaw timings and provide details via email
- Anna to outline standard conditions for noise exemptions including:
  - Permitted decibel levels
  - Duration restrictions
  - Directional requirements

### Sound Placement Requirements:

- Amplified sound must be positioned away from Ontario Street residences
- Speakers to be directed towards lake
- Town is very cautious about sound placement in Bronte Heritage Waterfront Park

## 8. Vendor Requirements and Regulations

### Food Vendors - Specific Requirements:

#### Business Licensing (Mandatory):

- All food vendors must have valid Town of Oakville business license
- Licenses obtained through Municipal Enforcement Services
- **Recommended:** Engage vendors who already have Oakville licensing
- **Alternative:** Vendors from other municipalities (Toronto, Mississauga, etc.) can obtain:
  - Temporary license specific to this event
  - Reduced cost compared to annual license

#### Health Regulations:

- All food vendors must comply with Halton Region Public Health requirements
- Town to provide details on health requirements via email

#### Food Vendor Insurance:

- Certificate of insurance required to obtain business license
- Handled through licensing process with Municipal Enforcement

### Non-Food Vendors:

- Town of Oakville business licensing NOT required
- Vendors can showcase and sell products without local licensing
- Examples: Running shoes, athletic gear, wellness products, etc.

### Vendor Insurance - General:

- Organizers have choice for non-food vendor insurance:
  - **Option 1:** Organizers add all vendors to their own certificate of insurance
  - **Option 2:** Require each vendor to provide individual certificate of insurance
- Recommendation: Organizers should decide their liability risk management approach

## 10. Tent and Installation Regulations

### General Staking Policy:

- **Town of Oakville does NOT permit any staking into park grounds**
- Applies to ALL installations: tents, inflatables, structures, vendor booths

### Tent Requirements by Size:

#### Small Pop-Up Tents (10' x 10'):

- Must be weighted down
- Acceptable weighting methods:
  - Commercial tent weights
  - Sandbags
  - Other secure weighting systems

#### Large Tents (20' x 20' and larger):

- Concrete weights required
- Building permits required
- **Recommendation:** Avoid large tents if possible to reduce complexity and permitting requirements

### Vendor Booth Setup:

- All vendor stands/tents must be weighted (no staking)
- Each vendor responsible for proper weighting of their setup

## 16. Event Size and Scalability Considerations

### Organizer Question:

- Any limits on number of vendors?
- Current estimate: 30+ vendors

### Town Response - Factors to Consider:

#### Physical Capacity:

- Park can only accommodate certain number of vendors, tents, and activities
- Must factor in multiple zones:
  - Performance/music area
  - Start/finish line congregation areas
  - Food vendor placement
  - Kids' activity zones
  - General vendor areas

#### First Year Event Considerations:

- Recommended to keep scalable for year one
- Better to plan conservatively and grow in future years
- Allows for:
  - Testing of plan
  - Assessment of success
  - Learning what works and what doesn't
  - Building on success in subsequent years

#### Organizational Complexity:

- More vendors = more complicated logistics
- Load-in/load-out becomes more complex
- Coordination requirements increase

### Town Recommendation:

- Keep it manageable and scalable for first year
- Focus on success rather than maximum size
- Allow room for growth in future years
- Final number dependent on site map review
