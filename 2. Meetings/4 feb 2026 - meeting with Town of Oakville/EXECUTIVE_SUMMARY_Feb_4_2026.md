## Important Requirements to Note

### No Staking Policy (CRITICAL):
- **NO stakes permitted** in park grounds
- All tents, inflatables, vendor booths must be weighted (sandbags, concrete weights)

### Food Vendors:
- Must have Town of Oakville business license (temporary or annual)
- Must comply with Halton Region Public Health requirements
- Insurance required as part of licensing process

### Noise Exemption:
- Amplified sound needed from 7:00 AM (bylaw timing TBD)
- Exemption will be written into Special Event Permit
- Sound must be directed toward lake (away from Ontario Street residences)
