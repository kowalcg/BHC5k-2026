## Potential Objections & Responses

### Objection: "We have limited marketing capacity"
**Response:** *"We completely understand. We're not asking you to do heavy lifting—even just sharing our posts on social media and allowing us to post on the gazebo board would be incredibly valuable. Anything beyond that is a bonus."*

### Objection: "BIA members might not see value in a running event"
**Response:** *"This is actually a full-day family festival, not just a race. The race ends by 9:30 AM, then thousands of families are at the waterfront all day. It's about community celebration on Father's Day—and that benefits restaurants, cafes, shops, and family-oriented businesses."*

### Objection: "Too close to event date to promote effectively"
**Response:** *"We're actually 4 months out, which gives us good lead time. Most race registrations happen in the final 6-8 weeks anyway. And for vendors and sponsors, businesses often make decisions on shorter timelines. The timing works."*

### Objection: "What if the event doesn't succeed in year one?"
**Response:** *"We're being realistic and conservative with year one expectations. Even if we hit 500 participants instead of 800, that's still significant for an inaugural event. And we're committed to growing it year over year. Early BIA support helps ensure success."*

### Objection: "BIA budget constraints for partnership"
**Response:** *"We're not asking for financial contribution from the BIA. The most valuable support you can provide is promotional—social media, business connections, and community outreach. Those don't have budget impact but have huge value for us."*
