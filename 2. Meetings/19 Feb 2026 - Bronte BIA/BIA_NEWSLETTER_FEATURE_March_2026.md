# Celebrate Father's Day at the Inaugural Bronte Harbour Classic 5K
## New Oakville Tradition Comes to Bronte Harbour Park - June 21, 2026

**Mark your calendars, Bronte!** This Father's Day, our community welcomes a brand-new tradition: the **Bronte Harbour Classic 5K**, featuring a scenic waterfront race, Kids 1K Fun Run, and an all-day family festival right here at beautiful Bronte Harbour Park.

### A Father's Day Like No Other

Sunday, June 21, 2026 will transform Bronte Harbour into a celebration of family, fitness, and community spirit. Whether you're a serious runner, casual walker, or just looking for a perfect Father's Day outing, this inaugural event offers something for everyone.

**Event Highlights:**
- **5K Waterfront Race** - Scenic course along Oakville's stunning lakeshore (8:00 AM start)
- **Kids 1K Fun Run** - Non-competitive run for ages 2-12 (perfect for the whole family!)
- **All-Day Festival** - Live music, vendor expo, family activities, and awards ceremony
- **Supporting Our Community** - Proceeds benefit Oakville Dads Charity

### Who's Behind This?

The Bronte Harbour Classic is organized by the passionate members of **Bronte Runners Club**, in partnership with experienced race director Greg Pace, who has been organizing the beloved Moon in June race for 35 years.

This team knows how to create memorable community events. Last year alone, the Bronte Runners organizing group raised **$27,000 for the Terry Fox Run** - the largest Oakville fundraising group. That same dedication and community spirit is going into making the Bronte Harbour Classic an annual tradition our families will cherish for years to come.
