### What We'd Love to Discuss on Tuesday

Here are the key areas where we think BIA partnership could create real value:

#### 1. **Marketing & Promotional Support**
- Social media promotion across BIA channels (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter)
- Event listing on Bronte BIA website
- Features in BIA newsletters and email communications
- Access to post on the community notice board at the gazebo
- Cross-promotion with Bronte business community

#### 2. **Business Community Engagement**
- Introductions to BIA member businesses who might be interested in:
  - **Sponsorship opportunities** (packages ranging from $850-$30,000)
  - **Vendor expo booths** ($250 for 10x10 space at all-day festival)
  - **Swag bag sponsorships** ($250 to reach all 800+ participants)
  - **Corporate team participation** (15% discount for teams of 6+)
- Guidance on which businesses would be the best fit
- Help us understand how to approach BIA members effectively

#### 3. **Community & Neighborhood Outreach**
- Help spread the word through neighborhood networks
- Connect us with community groups and organizations
- Position this as a "hometown event" for Bronte residents
- Encourage local participation and support

#### 4. **BIA Recognition & Partnership**
- We'd like to recognize BIA's support through:
  - Logo on event website and promotional materials
  - Social media mentions and recognition
  - Acknowledgment at race start and awards ceremony
  - Whatever makes sense for the BIA's goals

### Why This Matters for Bronte Businesses

I know you're focused on what benefits BIA members, so here's the business case:

**800+ race participants + thousands of festival visitors = significant foot traffic on Father's Day**

- Families will be in Bronte all day (7 AM - 3 PM)
- Race finishes by 9:30 AM, then festival activities until afternoon
- That means breakfast, brunch, lunch, coffee, shopping, and services
- Active, affluent families with disposable income
- Perfect demographic for restaurants, cafes, retail, and family services

**Plus, this is Year 1 of what we envision as an annual Father's Day tradition.** Businesses that get involved as founding partners will be part of building something special that grows year over year.

### What We're Bringing to the Table

- **Comprehensive partnership packages** at multiple price points
- **Low-cost entry options** ($250 vendor booths, $250 swag bags)
- **Professional event organization** with Town approval and CIBC as founding sponsor
- **Community-rooted approach** (we're Bronte Runners Club founders and local residents)
- **Charity component** supporting Oakville Dads Charity
- **Long-term vision** for annual event that benefits Bronte

We'd love to hear your thoughts on:

1. What type of promotional support makes sense for the BIA?
2. Which BIA member businesses do you think would be most interested?
3. What's the best approach to reach BIA members about opportunities?
4. Are there any BIA initiatives or campaigns we could tie into?
5. What would make this partnership most valuable for the BIA and its members?
