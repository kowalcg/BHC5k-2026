# Vendor Expo — Bronte Harbour Classic 5K
## Father's Day, June 21, 2026 | Bronte Harbour Park

The Bronte Harbour Classic isn't just a race. It runs from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, and it's open to the whole community — families, spectators, people who came for the live music and stayed for the afternoon. The vendor expo runs all day alongside it.

We're expecting 800+ registered race participants, plus festival visitors throughout the day. Father's Day tends to bring families out, and the waterfront setting at Bronte Harbour Park draws a lot of people who didn't sign up to run but show up anyway.

---

## What a Vendor Booth Includes

**$250 for the day**

- 10' x 10' booth space in the vendor expo area
- Full day access from setup (5:00 AM) through teardown (by 4:00 PM)
- Listed on the event website and race day program
- Option to include materials in participant swag bags at no extra charge (see note below)

The swag bag inclusion is worth mentioning — every registered runner gets a bag. If you have flyers, coupons, or samples you want in there, vendors can add them at no additional cost. It's a nice extra reach without anything more from you.

---

## What You Bring

- Your own 10' x 10' tent (required — event runs rain or shine)
- Tables, chairs, displays, and product inventory
- Proof of business insurance
- Your own payment processing

## Event Timeline

- **5:00–7:00 AM** — Vendor setup
- **7:00–8:00 AM** — Participants arriving, picking up race kits
- **8:00–9:30 AM** — Race in progress; spectators and families at the festival
- **9:30 AM–3:00 PM** — Post-race crowd, awards ceremony, peak traffic
- **By 4:00 PM** — Teardown complete

---

## Who's Coming

The crowd at Bronte Harbour on Father's Day skews active Oakville families — people who care about health, outdoor activities, and supporting local. The event is supporting Oakville Dads Charity, which adds another layer of community draw. If your business serves Oakville families, it's a good fit.

We're prioritizing food and beverage, health and wellness, family services, artisan goods, and local retail. Space is limited to 30–50 vendors, reviewed as applications come in.
