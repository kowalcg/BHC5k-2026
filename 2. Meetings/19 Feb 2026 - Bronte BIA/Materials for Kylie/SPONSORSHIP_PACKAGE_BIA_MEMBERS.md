# Sponsorship Opportunities — Bronte Harbour Classic 5K
## Father's Day, June 21, 2026 | Bronte Harbour Park, Oakville

The Bronte Harbour Classic is an inaugural Father's Day race and community festival organized by Bronte Runners Club. We're expecting 800+ registered participants, a full-day vendor expo, live music, kids activities, and an open festival at one of the best waterfront spots in Oakville.

CIBC is our founding sponsor. On Running is on board for elite prizes. The Town of Oakville has given official support. The Bronte BIA is a community partner.

This is Year 1. Sponsorship rates are set at inaugural pricing — they'll increase in future years, and founding sponsors lock in first right of refusal at current rates.

**Supporting:** Oakville Dads Charity (local — all donations stay in the community)
