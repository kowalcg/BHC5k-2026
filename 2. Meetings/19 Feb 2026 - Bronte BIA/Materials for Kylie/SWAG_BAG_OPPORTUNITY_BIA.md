Every registered participant in the Bronte Harbour Classic receives a race swag bag. We're expecting 800+ runners. Swag bag sponsorship puts your business directly into the hands of every single one of them.

**Cost: $250**

## What You Get

- Your materials in 800+ participant bags
- Your business listed as a Swag Bag Sponsor on the event website and race day program
- Social media recognition on event channels

At $250 for 800+ impressions, it works out to around $0.31 per person — with a physical item that goes home with them rather than getting scrolled past.

## What to Include

Whatever you want. Coupons, product samples, a business card with an offer on it, a QR code for something worth clicking — whatever makes sense for your business. The only ask is that it's something of actual value rather than just a plain flyer, and that it's family-appropriate.

Most businesses go with a discount offer or a free trial of some kind. Something that gives a first-time customer a reason to walk in the door.

You provide 850 items (800 bags plus a small buffer) and deliver them to us by the deadline we'll confirm when you sign up.
