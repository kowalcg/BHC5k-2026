- **Event hours:** 7:00 AM - 3:00 PM
- **Race start:** 8:00 AM
- **Street closure:** 7:00 AM

### Race Components
**5K Adult Race:**
- Scenic waterfront course
- First year event (future expansion to 10K planned)
- 10K route would extend to Appleby College area (Rebecca, Fourth Line loop)
- Elite runner prizes and competition

**Kids 1K Fun Run:**
- Loop within park grounds
- Ages 2-12
- Family-focused Father's Day activity

### All-Day Festival (Open to Public)
**Key Differentiator:** Extended dwell time model - unlike typical race formats where participants leave immediately post-event, BHC 5K creates all-day destination experience

**Festival Components:**
- Vendor expo (30-50 booths - see Section 4)
- Live music programming
- Family activities (mascots, face painting, jumping castles)
- Awards ceremony with professional MC
- Open to general public (not limited to race participants)

**Economic Impact Strategy:**
Drive extended visitor time in Bronte business district throughout day (7 AM - 3 PM), creating multiple purchase opportunities (breakfast, lunch, coffee, retail, services)

## 10. Route Planning & Logistics Update

### Potential Route Change
**Issue Identified:**
- Anna Rookyard (Town of Oakville) informed Greg of construction in Coronation Park
- **Location:** East parking lot
- **Timing:** Scheduled during race time (June 21)

**Impact on Route:**
- Original plan: Straight through Coronation Park
- May need to route around construction area ("around the building, the shores")
- Still accessing Coronation Park, just modified path

### Current Route Strategy
**Clockwise Start:**
- Race starts at 8:00 AM heading clockwise
- Runners return opposite direction
- **Benefit:** Street reopens immediately after start wave passes
- Minimizes closure duration and impact

**Geographic Points:**
- Marine Drive → Coronation Park → Return via different streets
- Original plan: Marine Drive → Jones → Lakefront
- Now: May need to loop around Lakeshore construction area

### Future Route (10K Expansion)
- Extend to Appleby College area
- Route through Rebecca and Fourth Line
- Return to Bronte
- **Requirement:** Nearly complete Lakeshore closure
- **Timeline:** Once 5K concept proven and established
