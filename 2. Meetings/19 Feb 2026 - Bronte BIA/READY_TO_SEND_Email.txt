OUR VISION: AN ATHLETIC EVENT DRIVING BUSINESS GROWTH & COMMUNITY IMPACT

We're not just organizing a race—we're creating a platform that:
• Promotes sports, running, and active healthy lifestyles
• Helps Bronte businesses thrive through regional exposure
• Raises substantial funds for local community needs through Oakville Dads Charity
• Celebrates Father's Day & the First Day of Summer with an unforgettable festival experience

Here's what we're building:

**1. Bringing People to Bronte from Across the Region**
• 800+ race participants + thousands of festival visitors on Father's Day
• Not just local residents—we're promoting throughout Oakville and neighboring cities (Burlington, Mississauga, Toronto)
• This is a DESTINATION event that brings people TO Bronte who might not normally visit
• All-day event (7 AM - 3 PM) = breakfast, lunch, coffee, shopping, services
• Active, affluent families with purchasing power discovering Bronte for the first time

**2. Showcasing Local Businesses to the Community**
Here's what excites us most: Many Bronte residents don't know all the amazing businesses in their own community. This event is an opportunity to connect them.

• Vendor Expo Marketplace (30-50 booths) where businesses can display products, demonstrate services, and meet hundreds of potential customers face-to-face
• It's not just about race day—it's about building lasting customer relationships
• Residents discovering businesses they didn't know existed, right in their neighborhood

**3. Creating Business Visibility Opportunities**
Multiple ways for businesses to participate and get noticed:
• Vendor booths ($250) - showcase products/services all day
• Sponsorships (starting from $850...) - brand visibility on race materials, t-shirts, banners, social media
• Swag bags ($250) - reach all 800+ participants directly with product samples/offers
• This is Year 1 of an annual tradition—founding partners get in on the ground floor

**4. Showcasing Bronte as a Regional Destination**
This is our chance to show the rest of Oakville and the region how beautiful Bronte is:
• Our stunning waterfront parks and trails
• The vibrant business community and local character
• All the amazing things to do and see in Bronte
• Put Bronte on the map as THE place to be on Father's Day

**5. Creating a Festival Atmosphere & Summer Celebration**
This isn't just a race—it's a full-day Father's Day celebration and the perfect way to kick off summer:
• **Fun for the whole family:** Jumping castles, face painting, activities for kids all day long
• **Live entertainment:** Local bands playing under the Sails—dance, enjoy the music, celebrate together
• **Food & drink:** Eat, drink, and soak in the beautiful marina atmosphere
• **Father's Day celebration:** Families spending quality time together in one of Bronte's most beautiful settings
• **First Day of Summer:** What better way to welcome summer than at the waterfront with music, food, and community?
• All-day festival creates extended opportunities for businesses—breakfast, brunch, lunch, shopping, and services

**6. Building Community Ownership & Excitement**
• Create an event that Bronte residents feel is THEIRS—an annual tradition they're proud of
• Get the community excited about showing off who we are and what we do
• Involve local businesses in creating something special together
• Year 1 is about building foundation; Year 5+ is about legacy

**7. Promoting Sports, Athletics & Active Lifestyle**
• Encouraging people to run, move, participate in sports
• Athletic event bringing the community together
• Sports and entertainment promoting healthy, active families
• Healthy, engaged community = thriving local businesses

**8. Raising Money for Local Community Needs**
This is a BIG part of our event—partnering with **Oakville Dads Charity** to raise substantial funds for our community:
• Food banks for families in need
• Scholarships for local kids
• Supporting where the need is greatest in our town
• **Keeping the money LOCAL—raised in Oakville, stays in Oakville, helps Oakville residents**
• Corporate social responsibility opportunity for business sponsors
• Feel-good story that businesses can be proud to support
