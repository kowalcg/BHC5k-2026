## THE BIG IDEA

Father's Day. Oakville's waterfront. A community that shows up for each other.

The Oakville Dads aren't just our charity — they're 60+ volunteers who will be on that course at 4 AM making race day happen for every runner, every family, every finish line moment. And the event they're helping build is one their whole family should be at. That story is our content backbone.

**Tagline thread to run through all content:**
> *"Oakville's race for Oakville families."*

**Posting philosophy for Oakville Dads group:**
The Dads group has ~6,000 active Oakville members. Their audience is our audience. Rather than treating them as a donation channel, treat them as the primary community amplifier. Posts should cover the full event experience — not just charity, not just running. What's in it for their family on June 21? What's in it for them as volunteers? What can they be proud of? Give them 9 posts across 15 weeks — roughly every other week — each with a clear, different angle.

## OPEN QUESTIONS FOR OAKVILLE DADS — LET'S HEAR FROM YOU

This plan is a starting point, not a ceiling. We want to push this event hard — digitally, physically, and through every channel available to both sides. This is a partnership, and the most successful events we've seen are the ones where both teams bring their networks to the table fully.

**We're asking Jeremy, Per, Andrew, and Brent directly:**

### 1. What have you done before that worked?
The Oakville Dads Community Fund has been active and growing for years. You've had events, campaigns, and drives that got people engaged. What has worked best — whether a specific post format, a time of year, a way of asking, or a channel that your group responds to?

We want to know what moves the needle for your members, so we don't reinvent the wheel.

### 2. Beyond the Facebook group — what other channels does the Oakville Dads community have?
The 9 Facebook posts above are just the group. But we're asking: what else?

- **Email list** — Do you have one? Could you send a dedicated BHC feature to it in April and June?
- **WhatsApp groups** — Are there sub-groups, neighbourhood chapters, or smaller circles where this could spread?
- **Existing events** — Do you have regular meetups, BBQs, or community nights between now and June where we could have a presence, hand out posters, or make a short announcement?
- **Community board access** — Rec centres, schools, local businesses, church halls — places you already have relationships?
- **Member businesses** — Are there Oakville Dads members who own local businesses and could post a flyer in their window or share with their customer base?

### 3. Physical outreach — what can we do together?
We will be doing physical poster and flyer distribution across Oakville, Bronte, and Burlington. But we want to go further:

- Can Oakville Dads members help distribute race posters in their neighbourhoods, at their workplaces, or at their kids' schools?
- Are there any community events between March and June where we could have a joint presence — a table, a banner, a five-minute announcement?
- Are there running or fitness groups connected to your members that we haven't thought of?

### 4. What does your group NOT respond well to?
Equally important — if there are tones, ask styles, or post types that tend to flop in your group, tell us now. We'd rather know upfront than learn it in May.

### 5. Who else should we be talking to?
Is there a community leader, a business, a school, a local media contact, or a running club in the Oakville area that you have a direct relationship with — someone who could amplify this event in a way we couldn't reach on our own?

### 5b. Sponsorship suggestions — we want your eyes on this.
You know the Oakville business community better than most. If you come across a company, brand, or local business that you think would be a natural fit for this event — whether it's a health and wellness brand, a family-focused business, a financial services firm, a real estate company, a local restaurant group, or anyone else who wants to be associated with Father's Day and community — tell us.

We're actively pursuing sponsors and every warm lead or suggestion helps. You don't need to make a pitch on our behalf — just flag the name and we'll take it from there. If you have a contact there, even better.

Current open sponsorship levels: Bronze ($850), Silver ($2,500), Gold ($5,000), Title ($8,000). There are also lower-entry options like vendor booths ($250) and swag bag inserts ($200) that are accessible to smaller local businesses.

Any suggestion is welcome — no lead is too small or too obvious.

### 6. Your network is our biggest untapped asset — and we're asking you to use it.
This is a direct ask, and we want to be honest about why we're making it.

The Oakville Dads community is made up of people who are connected — to local businesses, to municipal politics, to community organizations, to corporate decision-makers. Many of your members have relationships that we simply don't have and can't build in 107 days.

**Here's what we're asking:**

Can Oakville Dads members personally reach out — by email, by phone, by text — to anyone in their network who might:

- **Sponsor the event** at any level ($850 Bronze up to $8,000 Title) — local businesses, regional companies, anyone who wants to be associated with Oakville's Father's Day race
- **Make a personal donation** to the Oakville Dads Community Fund through the race donation link — even $100 or $250 from a business contact makes a difference and moves the fundraising total publicly
- **Share the event** directly with their customers, employees, or community contacts — a personal forwarded email from someone you trust converts better than any ad we could run

**The warm introduction model works like this:**
A short personal email from a member to their contact — "Hey, I'm involved with this Father's Day race at Bronte Harbour. It benefits our fund and it's a great community event. Here's how to get involved or donate." That's it. We provide the links. They provide the relationship.

**Political and municipal connections:**
If any members have relationships with local councillors, the mayor's office, MPPs, or Town of Oakville staff — a personal conversation about the event could open doors to additional municipal support, cross-promotion through Town channels, or visibility at public events leading up to June 21.

**We will provide:**
- A short, ready-to-forward email template they can personalize and send to their contacts
- Sponsorship package PDF (one page — easy to attach)
- Direct donation link
- Registration link

All they need to bring is the relationship. We handle the rest.
